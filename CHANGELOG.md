# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here, following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Each recording the 3D view shows is fitted to the app volume once: the first recording with content takes `--center` and `--scale` when either is given, and every other one, such as a recording opened with File > Open, is centred on its scene bounds (the 3D view's region of interest) once they settle, its largest extent 0.7 of the volume's smallest side. A recording keeps its fit as data streams in and gets it back when shown again; every fit is logged with its source and numbers. `--center` and `--scale` are optional, and `--auto-fit off` places every recording with them ([ADR-0092](docs/decisions/ADR-0092%20Each%20Recording%20Is%20Fitted%20To%20The%20App%20Volume%20And%20The%20App%20Scale%20Is%20The%20User's%20Zoom.md)).

- `--move-far-panels` (with `--panels`, off by default): a reattached panel whose centre is farther than 1.5 m from the eyes of the head that places panels is moved to where new panels open ([ADR-0095](docs/decisions/ADR-0095%20A%20Far%20Reattached%20Panel%20Is%20Moved%20Only%20When%20Asked.md)).
- `--reset-panels` (with `--panels`): each panel the viewer claims is closed first (`qar_gui_panels_close_panel`), and claimed once a listing shows it closed, so it is created afresh where new panels open, at the run's size ([ADR-0090](docs/decisions/ADR-0090%20A%20Reattached%20Panel%20Is%20Moved%20When%20Its%20Held%20Place%20Is%20Of%20No%20Use.md)).
- A reattached panel whose held place is of no use to the user is moved to where a new panel opens, with `set_pose` and `set_size`: its centre nearer than 0.2 m to the eyes of the head that places panels, or its size not the run's. A panel at the run's size stays where the user left it, however far away. The log names each reattached panel's held place, size and distance from the eyes, and why it stays or moves (ADR-0090).
- A panel closed while the viewer runs, by a mixer (Quaternar#344) or in the Hub Display, is seen at once through a state subscription (`qar_gui_panels_subscribe_panel_updates`) and reopened once a target's head streams after the close: a headset that drops out and comes back, or a mixer that fails and restarts, gets its panels back within the run. QAROS reports no proposer to an app, so a close in the Hub Display is undone too, and the log says so. A panel is reopened at most three times a run (ADR-0091, amends ADR-0090).

- The app volume is hidden at Stop and shown again at the next start, as the panels are: the viewer removes every peer that uses the volume (`qar_app_volumes_update_used_by_peers`), so no headset draws its box or content, and leaves it open, so the Hub keeps it with its place. The next run takes it over by `--volume-name` with that place, and each render sender lists its headset again, which shows it. The viewer never closes it (ADR-0093). `--reset-placement` also sets a taken-over volume to the flags' placement; the remembered place applies when QAROS creates the volume afresh (ADR-0089, amends ADR-0079 and ADR-0085).
- The app volume starts where it was last placed: the viewer keeps each volume's last pose and app pose, per `--volume-name`, in `qaros-rerun.volume-placements.json` next to the onboarding id file, written within a second of a move and at Stop, and asks QAROS for the volume there at the next start while `--origin` and `--yaw` are the ones it was moved from. The demos keep their place across runs. The app scale always starts at `--scale`. `--reset-placement` starts at the flags' place and forgets the kept one; an unreadable file is logged and ignored (ADR-0085, amends ADR-0066).
- New panels are placed for a standing user: every top edge at least 0.30 m below the eyes, centres between chest and waist, each panel tilted to face the eyes. The timeline stands straight ahead, nearest and lowest (0.40 m ahead, 0.50 m below the eyes); the views stand 0.45 m away beside it, alternately right and left. `--panel-posture sitting` keeps the earlier desk placement. Panels that exist keep their place; close them in the Hub Display once to have them placed anew (ADR-0083, amends ADR-0070 and ADR-0072).
- CI uploads the Windows x64 release viewer (`qaros-rerun.exe`, `log-test-scene.exe`) with the demo launchers and a tester README as the artifact `qaros-rerun-windows-x64-<commit>`, kept for 14 days, on every pull request and `main` run. [Installation](docs/INSTALLATION.md#download-a-build) says how to download it.
- `docs/quick-start.md`: a Rerun project in a QAROS headset in five steps, from the build to fitting the data on the table, with a symptom table.
- A one-hand grab moves the scene and a two-hand spread scales it, through QAROS's default gesture rules on the app volume. The viewer follows the volume's live pose, app pose and app scale: read at connect, on every volume update, every frame while a gesture runs and at least every 100 ms, and carried with each frame on both frame paths, so depth and the near plane stay in room metres at any scale. Every gesture on the volume is logged (ADR-0066, ADR-0080). In the glasses on 0.9.0-dev.3184 the grab works; the spread ends within 80 ms and barely scales ([Known issues](docs/known-issues.md)).
- Stream panels: each 2D view's GUI panel shows a CPU video stream the viewer owns (`QarVideoSender` with `QarGuiPanelStreamIdExt`), so the mixer samples the viewer's pixels as they are and no port is opened. Views are capped at 1280 px a side, padded to a width that is a multiple of 64 and a height that is a multiple of 8 (a test for Quaternar#343), and shown when they change, and once a second otherwise. Panels take names of their own (`<view>.stream.<volume>.rerun`) and are pointed at each run's streams with `qar_gui_panels_set_stream` on reattach (ADR-0076, ADR-0077).
- A timeline panel per viewer with `--panels`, in every demo whether or not it has 2D views, opening 0.5 m in front of the head that places the panels and 0.2 m below its eyes, tilted to face them. The viewer draws it itself, with Rerun's style and icons, and takes taps and drags on it as QAROS panel gestures: a tap seeks and pauses, a drag scrubs under the finger, and the button plays or pauses and shows the time control's play state. The thumb follows the viewer's time cursor, during playback and when the desktop time bar moves, and stays under the finger for a second after the last seek. One drag owns the cursor at a time, per peer and gesture id. The timeline is drawn only when it changes, and its GPU readback is collected on a later frame, so the UI thread never waits for the GPU (ADR-0078).
- Stop logs every teardown step's duration, and a step of its own closes the stream panels' gesture subscriptions and senders after the hide and before the session (ADR-0079).
- `docs/known-issues.md`: why panels need 0.9.0-dev.3177, three QAROS issues seen on that build (Quaternar#343, Quaternar#344, Quaternar#345), and GUI panel queries that block for about 2 s there. The viewer lists panels only to claim them, and one listing serves every claim for 30 s.
- A warning at start when the QAROS runtime is older than 0.9.0-dev.3177, the oldest the viewer supports.
- `docs/known-issues.md`: QAROS behaviour the viewer works around, with symptom, cause, workaround, the build it was seen on and its status.
- Architecture decisions on panel placement within reach of a headset (ADR-0070), the headset chosen by device category (ADR-0071), the timeline panel (ADR-0072), the panel listing retry (ADR-0073), local-app onboarding (ADR-0074) and closed panels claimed again by name (ADR-0075); panels claimed by common name (ADR-0054), the user lifecycle (ADR-0055) and the pairing-code fallback (ADR-0056) are published. ADR-0049's pose rule is superseded by ADR-0070, and the ADRs on depth and color, URI panels, the Vulkan path, the launch contract, target discovery, the session adapter, begin-frame polling, sender creation and the queue lock cite the current QAROS headers and docs.

### Changed

- The app volume is 1.5 m on each side by default (`--volume-size 1.5,1.5,1.5`), so a scene fits a headset's view from the table. The demos shrink with it to three quarters of their size, and air traffic moves into the default volume. A volume an earlier run left open at another size is resized to `--volume-size` when taken over, keeping its place and the user's zoom, and the log says so ([ADR-0094](docs/decisions/ADR-0094%20The%20Default%20App%20Volume%20Is%201.5%20m%20On%20Each%20Side.md)).
- The viewer never closes its app volume. A hide that cannot be completed at Stop leaves the volume open as it is and logs a warning naming the peers that may still see it, and the next start removes the peers a taken-over volume still lists before its senders list their headsets. The teardown step is named `hide volume` (ADR-0093).
- `docs/known-issues.md`: app volume listings that block for about 4 s, used-by removals of which only the first lands, and QAROS's depth check reporting a depth view that covers less than 1 % of the frame as never written, on both depth encodings.
- QAROS's app scale is only the user's zoom: it starts at 1, and the data scale and center stay in the viewer's data fit, which QAROS never sees. A two-hand spread scales the fitted scene around its own size at any data scale, and the reverse-infinite depth declaration is divided by the zoom alone. The zoom is remembered per volume with its place (between 0.1 and 10), a taken-over volume keeps it, and a taken-over app scale that is no zoom (outside 0.1 to 10, or the `--scale` earlier builds set) is set back to 1. `--reset-placement` starts at zoom 1 (ADR-0092, amends ADR-0066, ADR-0080, ADR-0084, ADR-0085 and ADR-0089).
- Every panel claim, a reattached panel's too, waits for the head that places panels, and finds the panel by the id QAROS derives from its common name, since a panel carried into a new Hub session is listed without its display name. With the claim's first frame the panel gets its size, the pose the claim moved it to, and the show again (ADR-0090).
- The eyes are drawn on a render thread of their own by default, the decoupled render loop, so head motion no longer waits for the scene update. `--inline-render` draws them inside the viewer frame instead. Without a sink, and with the QAROS Vulkan sink in a window on a GPU whose graphics queue family has one queue, the viewer draws inline with a warning that names the reason and the flags; `--decoupled-render` is still accepted and refuses to start in those cases instead. Every run logs how the eyes are drawn, for example `render: decoupled (raw batches on queue 1)` or `render: inline, because ...` (ADR-0088, amends ADR-0058 and ADR-0081).
- Depth goes to QAROS as Rerun draws it, infinite reverse-Z, declared as reverse-Z with an infinite far plane (`near_plane` 1e30, `far_plane` the near plane), so QAROS converts it once and the viewer no longer clamps content past `--far`: `--depth reverse-infinite`, the new default on both paths. `--depth forward`, `reverse` and `linear` remain as the fallback until a headset run confirms the default (ADR-0084, superseding ADR-0015's depth encoding).
- The QAROS Vulkan path's raw batches (acquire, release, discard signals, idle waits) go to a second queue of wgpu's queue family when the GPU has one, and the semaphores the handover stages on wgpu's queue hold whichever thread's submission takes them. `--decoupled-render` with the QAROS Vulkan sink then runs in a window, where a window resize parks the render thread between two eye frames while eframe reconfigures the surface; on a GPU whose graphics family has one queue it keeps the queue submission lock and ends with an error in a window (ADR-0081).
- A stream panel whose stream id QAROS cannot read is listed without it, so the other panels still open (QAROS 0.9.0-dev.3184 reports such a panel as "0 payload bytes").
- The QAROS Rust binding is pinned to QAROS `main` at `e0c0195a`, whose C header matches the 0.9.0-dev.3184 runtime apart from one added status code. Dropping a sender creation in flight returns at once; the session's teardown waits for its callback.
- Panels are listed through a raw listing that reads each panel's content type first, so a stream panel in the session no longer makes the listing fail.
- New panels open within reach of a headset: the viewer asks QAROS for each streaming peer's device category (`qar_peer_spec_get_category`) and places panels by the first `QAR_DEVICE_CATEGORY_HEADSET` head near the app volume. A Display Receiver (a Visualizer receiver) or a viewer whose category QAROS cannot say places them only when no headset streamed within 60 s of the first head near the volume. The log names the viewer, its peer and its device.
- `--launched-by-qaros` on Windows joins as a local app, as QAROS expects of an app it launches there, and reads no launch invite from stdin; stdin still signals Stop. Other platforms keep the stdin launch invite.
- The stop watchdog ends the process 600 ms after a stop request when the teardown has not, so with its bounded flush of at most 200 ms the exit and its log line land before QAROS kills the app, about 1 s after closing stdin.
- A failed panel listing opens no panel and is retried after 0.5 s, then every 30 s, instead of at every sync.

- `--panels` is a plain switch and needs `--sink qaros`; panels need QAROS 0.9.0-dev.3177 or later.

### Removed

- The web panel route and the panel server; panels are video streams. `--panel-route`, `--panel-base-url` and `--dead-panels` are gone, and `--panels` takes no address. QAROS 0.9.0-dev.3177 or later.

### Fixed

- A panel stream's first frame goes out within a few frames of the mixer connecting it: the viewer makes no listing, claim or sender replacement while a sender it just put in place has not shown its first frame (at most 3 s), and claims one panel per sync. The mixer holds its graphics queue until a new panel stream's first frame arrives, and the next panel's claim blocked that frame for about 2 s, past Windows' GPU timeout: the driver reset the mixer's device and the headset dropped, closing every panel, when a run reattached its panels within five minutes of the last ([ADR-0096](docs/decisions/ADR-0096%20No%20Claim%20Blocks%20A%20New%20Panel%20Stream's%20First%20Frame.md), amends ADR-0077 and ADR-0087).
- A Ctrl+C Stop hides the app volume before the stop watchdog ends the process, so its box and its last content leave the glasses. Stop reads nothing from QAROS: the volume's used-by peers are followed through its update subscription during the run, since each app volume listing blocks for about 4 s. Each peer is removed in a call of its own, 15 ms apart, as QAROS refuses all but the first of several removals sent together, and the hide is confirmed by the Hub's next bake of the volume within 180 ms; a peer the bake still lists is removed again ([ADR-0093](docs/decisions/ADR-0093%20The%20App%20Volume%20Is%20Hidden%20Within%20The%20Stop%20Budget%20And%20Never%20Closed.md), amends ADR-0089).
- A panel listed `Closed` when a run claims it, such as one a mixer closed after the last run hid it, is shown: QAROS creates it afresh, and the claim shows it with its stream and again with its first frame, at its held place when that place is of use and otherwise where new panels open. It stayed invisible for the run before (ADR-0091).
- A stream panel the claim shows is shown once more with its first frame. QAROS 0.9.0-dev.3184 refuses the claim's show as stale because it follows the claim's `set_stream`, and after a Stop and a Start in the same Hub session the view panels did not come back ([Known issues](docs/known-issues.md)).
- Resizing the desktop window no longer blanks the panels in the glasses. Each 2D view streams one frame per run, fixed from the first crop size that holds for 1 s, and every later crop is scaled into it on the CPU, aspect preserved and letterboxed in the view's background colour, so a resize never replaces a panel's stream. On QAROS 0.9.0-dev.3184 every replaced stream stayed blank (ADR-0087).
- Panels follow the active recording: when another recording is opened (for example from the command palette) or the blueprint drops or hides a 2D view, the panels of views no longer in the layout are hidden instead of staying open with their last image, and shown again when their views return. The new recording's 2D views get panels as before, and the timeline panel follows the active recording's time (ADR-0082).
- Both render senders declare premultiplied alpha (`ColorAlphaMode::Premultiplied`), so the mixer blends the stream by its coverage; an undeclared stream is drawn opaque, uncovered pixels included.

## [0.1.0]

Initial public release.

### Added

- `qaros-rerun`: the Rerun 0.40.0 viewer with a headset renderer. The 3D view is rendered once per eye of every user, with the eye poses and fields of view each user's QAROS render stream asks for, and handed over as color plus depth; pixels where nothing was drawn are transparent. Includes a headless mode and `log-test-scene`.
- QAROS sink (`--sink qaros`) over the public `qar-streaming-c` API: onboarding with a pairing code, as a local app (`--local-app`) or with a launch invite (`--launched-by-qaros`), an app volume with placement and scale, one render sender per target app found in the peer list, a render policy capping the users rendered at once (`--max-users`, default 2), and an orderly stop on a QAROS Stop, Ctrl+C or a closed console.
- Vulkan path (`--qaros-graphics vulkan`, the default on Windows): the viewer creates its own Vulkan device on the session's adapter and renders each eye straight into the images of the user's Vulkan render sender. The CPU path (`--qaros-graphics cpu`) reads the eyes back and copies them into a CPU render sender.
- 2D views as QAROS GUI panels (`--panels`): views are cropped from the viewer's UI, served by a local panel server and opened as panels within reach of the first viewer near the app volume, 0.8 m away and to the right of the gaze, with stable names across runs; after that the Hub keeps each panel where it is, or where a user moved it.
- Decoupled render loop (`--decoupled-render`, experimental): the eyes are drawn on a render thread from the last finished scene, at the sink's rate.
- `xr-math`: per-eye cameras from OpenXR poses and fields of view, the off-axis projection decomposed for `re_renderer`, depth encodings, view coordinates to room axes and app-volume placement.
- `frame-sink`: the `FrameSink` seam, users and render policy, the frame stamp, and a PNG image sink with simulated users and scripted head motion for runs without QAROS: a level circle walk, an orbit that keeps looking at the data, or standing still, with eye height, period and an optional camera-like field of view (`--motion`, `--eye-height`, `--period`, `--fov`).
- `vulkan-device`: an app-created Vulkan device with a physical device picker, extra device extensions, external image wrapping, semaphore staging and image hand-over batches.
- `panel-server`: a local HTTP server and page that show the latest frame of each 2D view.
- Patched copies of Rerun's `re_view_spatial` (a 3D view observer hook) and `re_renderer` (picking depth kept on the GPU, a second render context on a shared device, a queue submission lock).
- Demo launchers for Rerun examples (structure from motion, drone lidar, air traffic, robot arm) that record once and replay in a loop, placed and coloured for see-through glasses: centered on their data 0.5 m above a room origin on a table (`--origin` for an origin on the floor), sized to the app volume, light colours for what matters, the drone flight over a dim backdrop of the whole scan, the air traffic altitudes exaggerated 20x.
- Integration guide, design notes and architecture decision records under `docs/`.
